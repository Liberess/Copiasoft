Privacy Policy

App: Don't Pop!: Splash Race (Windows / Steam)

Developer: CopiaSoft · Contact: support@copiasoft.com

1. Overview

CopiaSoft publishes this Privacy Policy to explain how information is handled when users play Don't Pop!: Splash Race.

2. Information We Collect

The game does not require direct identifiers such as real name, address, or phone number. If a user contacts support, the inquiry may include an email address, message content, device information, and runtime environment details.

3. Steam Information

When the game is used through Steam, account, purchase, launch, achievement, and cloud save information may be processed by Valve Corporation under Steam's own policies.

4. Purpose of Use

Information is used only for support, troubleshooting, service improvement, and abuse prevention.

5. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell or arbitrarily share personal information with third parties except where required by law.

6. Retention and Deletion

Support inquiry information is deleted within a reasonable period after the purpose is fulfilled, unless retention is required by applicable law.

7. Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact support@copiasoft.com.
