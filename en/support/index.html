Contact Email

If you have any problems or questions while playing the game, please contact us at the email below.

support@copiasoft.com

If your mail app does not open, long-press the address above to copy it and send an email from your mail service directly.

Information to Include

Including the following information helps us respond faster and more accurately:

  1. Game name: Wallbreaker
  2. Your user ID or PlayFab ID (available in the in-game options)
  3. For payment issues, your Google Play order number (e.g., GPA.XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXXX)
  4. Date and time the problem occurred
  5. Description of the problem (what you did and what happened)
  6. Screenshots, if available

Common Topics

  • Payments / refunds: Refunds for in-app purchases follow the Google Play refund policy. See the Terms of Service for details.
  • Purchase restoration: Non-consumable products such as permanent ad removal can be restored from the restore purchases button in the in-game options.
  • Data deletion: See the Privacy Policy for how to request game data deletion.
